TENNIS H2H MODEL

Built around players who've met before. Log their past meetings, and the model blends that repeat history with current form to project sets won, total games over/under, and the full matchup simulation.

Model output from the data you enter \u2014 not sportsbook odds, and not betting advice. H2H on a small sample is noisy: two or three meetings tell you much less than eight. The app flags sample strength so you know how much to trust it. Gamble responsibly if at all.

HEAD-TO-HEAD MEETINGS
Enter each past meeting's set scores. This is what powers the repeat-data side of the model \u2014 real total-games history between these two is far better signal than a generic estimate.

Each player's serve-hold probability comes from what you enter, or is derived from world ranking on a log-scaled strength difference around a tour-typical baseline (about 80% hold on the ATP side, 65% on the WTA side), then adjusted for recent form and surface. Head-to-head shifts that hold gap, weighted by how many times they've actually met \u2014 one meeting barely moves it, five or more carries close to full weight, so a fluke result doesn't dominate. The match is then simulated 20,000 times game by game under real scoring rules, including two-game set margins and tiebreaks at 6-6, and every figure below is counted from those simulated outcomes rather than estimated separately \u2014 so the set and games numbers stay consistent with the match-winner number by construction. Where past meetings are logged, their actual average total games is blended into the games projection, with the same sample weighting. This is a hobby model for research, not financial or betting advice.
