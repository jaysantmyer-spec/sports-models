Live data from the MLB Stats API. Hitters are each team's top plate-appearance leaders on the active roster (lineups aren't finalized until close to game time, so this approximates the likely starters). Daily Projections ranks by projected hits + runs + RBI and shows the modeled chance of getting at least one hit. Prop win probabilities are modeled with a Poisson approximation around each player's projected rate. All of it is adjusted for opposing pitcher/lineup, approximate park factors, current venue weather (neutral for domed parks), and home/away. Common sportsbook lines are used for props (e.g. 0.5/1.5 hits) but real books may offer different lines, odds, and vig. This is a hobby stat model, not financial or betting advice — please gamble responsibly if at all.
