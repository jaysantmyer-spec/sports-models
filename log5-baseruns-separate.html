LOG 5 + BASERUNS

Two formulas, two jobs. Log 5 prices the moneyline. BaseRuns prices the totals. Every game today gets both, separately, with live data and full manual override.

LOG 5 → MONEYLINEAdjusted Pythagorean from runs scored and allowed, then P(A beats B) = A(1−B) ÷ [A(1−B) + (1−A)B]. Starter and home field applied.
BASERUNS → TOTALSDeserved runs per game for each offence and defence, adjusted for the opposing starter and park, simulated into a full-game total, team totals and a first-five total.

Bankroll note. Even a sound model loses roughly 40–45% of the time to variance. Never wager money you cannot afford to lose.

Log 5 and BaseRuns are implemented exactly as published and are never combined. Log 5 takes each team's Pythagorean win rate from actual runs scored and allowed, adjusted for the announced starter's FIP relative to league (scaled by typical start length, capped) and for home field as an odds shift after Log 5. BaseRuns is computed for each team's offence and for runs allowed; the per-game figures are scaled against league average and the opposing starter's FIP share to give each lineup's expected runs, which are simulated as Poisson draws for full-game, team and first-five totals. The sequencing signal is the gap between actual runs and BaseRuns: a team scoring well under its deserved figure is the source's totals edge. The Pythagorean exponent defaults to 1.83 (Pythagenpat), which grades better than 2.0 empirically; the squared form is one click away. Caught stealing and grounded-into-double-play totals missing from the pitching feed are estimated from at-bats so both sides stay symmetric; supplied values are never overwritten. Poisson slightly under-disperses real innings. Season stats for a past date include games after it, so grading is only honest for a slate run live.
