NBA PICK CRITIC

Second opinion on your last NBA Ensemble slate. It pulls the sportsbook lines ESPN publishes, puts the model next to the market, prices every side, and flags anything that makes a number less trustworthy.

How it judges. Market win chance is the moneyline with the vig removed. Spread cover and over/under chances use the model's margin and total with its own fitted residual spreads (σ), so a 2-point gap matters less in a game with a wide σ. A side is a play when the model's chance beats the price's implied chance by at least your edge threshold, a lean at half of that. When the model and market disagree by more than 6 points of margin or 10 of total, the gap usually means the market has news the model doesn't (late scratches, rest, a trade), so the side is marked check news instead of play. A moneyline edge is ignored when the model and market agree on the margin within 1.5 pts, since it then comes only from how the model converts a margin into a win chance. Lines can be edited inside each card. This is a research tool, not betting advice.
