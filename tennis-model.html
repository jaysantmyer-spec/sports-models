MATCH BREAKDOWNS

How it works: each player's serve-hold probability is either taken from what you enter or derived from world ranking (log-scaled strength difference around a tour-typical baseline \u2014 roughly 80% hold on the ATP side, 65% on the WTA side), then adjusted for recent form, head-to-head history, and surface. The match is then simulated 20,000 times game by game \u2014 real scoring rules including deuce-free game-level holds, 2-game set margins, and tiebreaks at 6-6 \u2014 and every market is counted from those simulated outcomes rather than estimated with a formula. That means set betting, total games, and player game totals are all internally consistent with the match-winner number by construction. Ranking-derived holds are a heuristic, not a measured stat: entering real hold percentages (widely published on tour stat sites) will meaningfully improve accuracy. This is a hobby model for research, not financial or betting advice.
