Live data from the MLB Stats API. Matchup factor blends the batter's season AVG with the opposing starter's average allowed specifically against that batter's throwing-hand side (falls back to the pitcher's overall average if the platoon split isn't available). OPS and WHIP are folded in as secondary quality multipliers (square-root dampened so they don't double-count with the matchup factor). Home/away and day/night use each player's own actual split stats when available, compared to their season baseline, rather than a flat assumption. Park factors are approximate reference values. Weather uses current conditions at the venue (neutral for domed parks). Hitters are each team's top plate-appearance leaders (lineups aren't finalized this far ahead). This is a hobby stats model, not financial or betting advice.
