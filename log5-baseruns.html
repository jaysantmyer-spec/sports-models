BASERUNS
Mimics a real game by separating an offence into runners reaching base, runners driven home, and home runs that clear the bases automatically. Unlike Runs Created, it does not assume events occur in a perfect order.
BaseRuns = A × BB + C + D
A (Baserunners) = Hits + Walks + Hit by Pitch − Home Runs
B (Advancement) = (1.4 × Total Bases) − (0.6 × Hits) − (3 × Home Runs) + (0.1 × Walks)
C (Outs) = At Bats − Hits + Caught Stealing + GIDP
D (Guaranteed) = Home Runs

Bankroll note. Even a mathematically sound model loses roughly 40–45% of the time to variance. Never wager money you cannot afford to lose.

BaseRuns and Log 5 are implemented exactly as specified, with no alteration to their coefficients or structure. Total bases allowed are reconstructed from hits, doubles, triples and home runs surrendered, since the pitching side does not publish the figure directly. Caught stealing and grounded-into-double-play totals default to zero when the source does not return them, which slightly understates the out component and therefore slightly overstates BaseRuns; both fields remain editable. The Adjusted Pythagorean step uses the squared form. A discrepancy between actual and deserved runs is a signal about sequencing luck, not a guarantee of regression, and the sample it rests on matters: a gap of a few runs across a full season is noise. Team statistics come from the MLB Stats API, which requires no key. Every field stays editable after loading, and each result shows its full arithmetic.
