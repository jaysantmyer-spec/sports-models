ETF FACTOR SCANNER

A composite bullish/bearish score for major ETFs, built from price momentum, sector business health, macro conditions (rates, inflation, jobs, GDP), and market psychology (VIX).

This produces a composite technical/fundamental score, not a predicted price target or guarantee \u2014 it reflects current conditions and momentum, which can change quickly. Not financial advice; I'm not a financial advisor. Always do your own research and consider consulting a licensed professional before investing.

RANKED: COMPOSITE SCORES
Paste both API keys, then tap Scan.

Momentum factor: 50-day vs 200-day moving average trend, 1-month price return, and RSI(14). Sector health factor: that day's performance of the ETF's mapped GICS sector (via FMP), used as a short-term proxy for aggregate earnings/business sentiment \u2014 broad-market and bond/commodity ETFs (SPY, QQQ, DIA, IWM, GLD, TLT) don't map to a single sector and skip this factor. Macro factor: recent trend in the Fed funds rate, CPI inflation, unemployment rate, and real GDP growth (via FRED), shared across all ETFs since these are market-wide conditions, with rate-sensitive ETFs (XLF, XLRE, TLT) weighted more toward the rate-trend sub-factor. Psychology factor: VIX level and its recent trend as a fear/complacency proxy. All factors are combined into a single weighted composite score \u2014 this is a simplified educational model, not a rigorous quant strategy, and it does not account for company-specific news, geopolitical events, or anything not reflected in the data pulled. Past patterns do not guarantee future results.
