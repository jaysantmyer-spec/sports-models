CFB VALUE FINDER

Scans real, live college football odds across dozens of US sportsbooks (via The Odds API) for games about to start, and ranks the biggest priced edges \u2014 where one book pays out more than the consensus fair line suggests.

This surfaces pricing gaps between real sportsbooks, not certainties. "Edge" is an estimate, not a guarantee \u2014 and even a real statistical edge can lose. Not betting advice. Gamble responsibly if at all.

Paste your API key above, then tap Scan.

Edge = the consensus "fair" probability (average of all books' odds after removing each book's built-in vig) minus the implied probability of the single best price found. A positive edge means at least one book is currently offering a better payout than the market as a whole thinks is warranted. This can reflect a genuine mispricing, a book being slow to update, or just normal market noise \u2014 it is not a prediction of who wins. Defaults to the NCAAF moneyline market; college football odds can be thin on FCS/smaller-conference games since fewer books post lines on them, which naturally produces fewer comparable edges. The time window filters to games whose scheduled kickoff falls in that range from the moment you scan. Uses your own free-tier API quota (usually 500 requests/month); each scan costs one request. This is a stats/odds-comparison tool, not financial or betting advice.
