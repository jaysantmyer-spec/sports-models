NBA FOUR FACTORS SIMULATOR

Every game simulated possession by possession from Dean Oliver's Four Factors, with fatigue, rest, referee tendency and home court layered on. Moneyline and total from the same simulation.

FOUR FACTORS — eFG% (40%), TOV% (25%), ORB%/DRB% (20%), FT Rate (15%), offence and defence, live from ESPN
POSSESSION ENGINE — each trip: turnover → foul trip → 2 or 3 attempt → make / miss → offensive rebound loop. Offence factor × defence factor ÷ league average, every step
GAME LAYERS — pace from both teams, in-game fatigue curve on transition defence, back-to-back penalty from the schedule, referee whistle multiplier on foul trips, home court
OUTPUT — 10,000 simulated games → win probability, margin, total distribution, team totals
SETTINGS
No API key. Season stats populate once the season is underway; until then loaded figures reflect the most recent completed season, and every input can be typed in by hand. Referee crews post a few hours before tip — set the whistle per game in the override panel.
Bankroll note. Even a sound model loses roughly 40–45% of the time to variance. Never wager money you cannot afford to lose.

Dean Oliver's Four Factors are computed exactly as published: eFG% = (FGM + 0.5×3PM)/FGA; TOV% = TOV/(FGA + 0.44×FTA + TOV); ORB% = ORB/(ORB + Opp DRB); DRB% = DRB/(DRB + Opp ORB); FT Rate = FTA/FGA. Defensive versions use the figures the team allows. The 40/25/20/15 weights appear in the Four Factors display as a composite for reference; predictions come from the possession simulation, not the composite. Each possession blends the offence's rate with the defence's allowed rate multiplicatively against league average, which is the standard approach. Possessions per team come from both teams' pace. The fatigue curve raises opponent shooting efficiency linearly through the game, reaching the stated bonus on the final possession, which approximates the described effect of tiring transition defence; it is a modelled shape, not measured player tracking. The back-to-back penalty (−1.5% eFG, +0.5% TOV) is applied when the schedule shows a game the previous day. Referee whistle scales the probability that a possession ends at the line. Home court is applied symmetrically, raising the home side's shooting efficiency and lowering the visitor's by the same amount, calibrated so the standard setting reproduces the real-world home win rate of roughly 57%. The named model this is inspired by was proprietary and unpublished; what is implemented here is the described structure — possession simulation, fatigue, rest, referee and rotation-sensitive pace — using public data. Coach-specific rotation equations are not reproducible without minutes data and are not claimed. When ESPN's season endpoint does not expose opponent totals, defensive factors fall back to league average and the card says so. This is a research tool, not betting advice.
