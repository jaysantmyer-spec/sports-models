MLB EV SCANNER

Quantitative slate scan. Projects fair lines from peripheral metrics, compares against live sportsbook odds, and returns only picks clearing a strict EV+ threshold.

OPERATING CONSTRAINTS
• Markets restricted to F5 moneyline, F5 totals, and team totals
• Maximum 2 picks per slate · exactly 1 pick per game
• EV+ must exceed the threshold or nothing is returned
• Zero market-flow input: no ticket %, no handle, no line movement
• Missing data is reported as unavailable, never estimated
CONFIGURATION
F5 and team-total markets are additional markets on The Odds API and may require a paid tier. If your key lacks access, the scan will report that explicitly rather than silently returning nothing.

Fair lines are projected from FIP computed directly from raw counting stats ((13·HR + 3·(BB+HBP) − 2·K)/IP + constant), which is the same figure published on public leaderboards. FIP rather than ERA drives the projection deliberately: the gap between them is the regression signal being traded. Run distributions are simulated 20,000 times per game as independent Poisson processes per half-inning block, with starter workload capped at five innings for F5 markets and bullpen innings modelled separately for full-game team totals. Opponent offence is scaled by runs per game against league average; park factors are approximate reference values, not measured current-season figures. Expected value is computed as (P_win × net profit) − (P_loss × stake) at the displayed price. Statcast expected-contact metrics are attempted and reported as unavailable when the source cannot be reached cross-origin. This is a research tool, not betting advice. No model output is a guarantee; variance dominates small samples.
