Open markets come from Kalshi's public API (no key). Forecast high comes from Open-Meteo at the official NWS station for each city. Model = normal distribution around the forecast (σ ${'3.2'}° base, wider with lead). Same-day markets use the observed high so far: the day's high can only go up from there. Confidence is capped at 75% until the graded log earns more. The market has live thermometers too — same-day "edges" are usually the model being late, not the market being wrong.
