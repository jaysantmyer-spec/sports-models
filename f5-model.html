F5 MODEL

Prices First 5 markets from predictive pitching metrics, handedness-split lineup strength, and park conditions.

1. PITCHING BASELINE — last 30 days weighted 60%, full season 40%, then:
Projected P_ERA = (xFIP × 0.5) + (SIERA × 0.5)

2. LINEUP SPLITS — wRC+ over last 30 days vs the opposing pitcher's hand. 100 is league average.

3. PARK & WEATHER — park factor, plus 2.5% run production per 10°F and wind effects.

4. CALCULATION — from a 2.60 run-per-team F5 baseline:
Runs = 2.60 × (wRC+ ÷ 100) × Park × (Opposing P_ERA ÷ 4.00)

Bankroll note. Even a mathematically sound model loses roughly 40–45% of the time to variance — umpire strike zones, sequencing, and defensive errors are outside any projection. Never wager money you cannot afford to lose.

The pitching baseline, lineup split, park adjustment and run calculation are implemented exactly as specified, including the 60/40 recency weighting, the equal xFIP and SIERA blend, the 2.60 run baseline and the 4.00 league-average ERA denominator. Weather is off by default so results match the specification's own worked example; when enabled, temperature applies 2.5% per 10°F relative to the 70°F reference and wind applies a run-production effect scaled from the stated home-run figure, which is an interpretation since home run probability and total runs are not the same quantity. Park factors shown on load are general reference values rather than Statcast five-inning figures, and remain editable. Schedule, starters, handedness and conditions come from the MLB Stats API and Open-Meteo, neither of which requires a key. Every field stays editable after loading.
