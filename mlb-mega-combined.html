Live data from the MLB Stats API. Hitters are each team's top plate-appearance leaders on the active roster (lineups aren't finalized until close to game time, so this approximates the likely starters). A hitter-quality factor is computed as AVG \u2212 OBP \u2212 SLG: since OBP and SLG normally exceed AVG, this number is negative for nearly every player, and a MORE negative total (OBP+SLG outpacing AVG by more than league-average) is treated as the better/stronger signal, reflecting more walks and extra-base power relative to raw average. That factor is blended into Daily Projections and all batter props alongside matchup, park factor, weather, and home/away. Prop win probabilities are modeled with a Poisson approximation around each player's projected rate. Park factors are approximate reference values; weather uses current venue conditions (neutral for domed parks). Common sportsbook lines are used for props (e.g. 0.5/1.5 hits) but real books may offer different lines, odds, and vig.
