TRAIN ON A SEASON — WALK-FORWARD
Walks the season in order. Team and starter stats are queried through the day before each game; historical weather comes from Open-Meteo's archive. Models refit on an expanding window and every scored prediction is out of sample. Roughly 3–5 minutes per 600 games.
Bankroll note. Even a sound model loses roughly 40–45% of the time to variance. The number to trust is the out-of-sample one this trainer reports, not any published claim.

Run engine: each team-side's first-five runs are modelled as negative-binomial with mean from a log-link regression on the team's BaseRuns per game, the opponent's BaseRuns-allowed per game, the opposing starter's FIP scaled by the share of five innings he typically covers, park factor, home indicator, and first-pitch temperature and wind speed. Coefficients are fit by Poisson maximum likelihood with light ridge shrinkage, and the negative-binomial shape by method of moments on the training residuals. F5 ratings: a Bradley–Terry model on which side led after five innings (ties excluded), with a home term and a term on the starters' FIP difference, fit by maximum likelihood with mean-gradient steps. Ensemble weights are Brier skill over a coin flip; a model that does not beat 0.235 gets no weight. A logit-linear calibration curve is fit on the out-of-sample ensemble predictions. Wind direction is not modelled because it requires each park's field orientation. Lineups are team-level; posted lineups are not yet used. Caught stealing and grounded-into-double-play totals absent from the pitching feed are estimated from at-bats. This is a research tool, not betting advice.
