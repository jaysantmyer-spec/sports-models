MLB MODEL BACKTEST

Replays the projection model across dates that already happened, grades every prop against the real box score, and reports which factors actually separated winners from losers.

Each day's projection is built only from stats through the day before that game, so no result leaks backwards into its own prediction. Everything below is measured from real outcomes, not estimated.
BACKTEST RANGE
Each day pulls a schedule, every box score, and pre-game stats for both teams. Expect roughly a minute per day \u2014 progress shows below.

Method: for each date, the schedule and final box scores are pulled from the MLB Stats API. Each hitter who actually appeared is projected using only their season stats through the previous day (via a date-ranged stats query), adjusted for the opposing starter, an approximate park factor, and home/away \u2014 then every prop is graded against what that player actually did. Weather is excluded from the backtest because historical conditions at first pitch aren't available from a free source, so its live-model contribution is untested here. Park factors are approximate reference values rather than measured current-season figures. Calibration compares the model's stated confidence against realised hit rate, which is the single most informative check: a well-calibrated 70% bucket should win close to 70% of the time, and a large gap in either direction means the model is over- or under-confident rather than simply lucky or unlucky. Sample sizes are shown throughout because a 100% hit rate on four bets means very little. This is a research tool, not betting advice.
