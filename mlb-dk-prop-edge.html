Model probability blends: batter AVG vs the opposing starter's average allowed against that batter's specific throwing-hand side (falls back to overall average if no platoon split is available), batter OPS and pitcher WHIP as secondary quality multipliers, each player's own home/away split, the pitcher's day/night split matched to today's actual game time, an approximate park factor, and current venue weather (neutral for domed parks). "Edge" is model probability minus DraftKings' implied probability from their price. Only DraftKings lines priced between -120 and -1200 are shown, per your request. Hitters are each team's top plate-appearance leaders (lineups aren't finalized this far ahead). Uses your own Odds API quota \u2014 this pulls DraftKings player props per game, which is quota-heavier than a plain odds scan, so expect it to use more of your monthly allowance per run.
