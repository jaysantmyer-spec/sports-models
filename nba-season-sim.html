NBA SEASON SIMULATOR

Plays out every remaining regular-season game thousands of times with the NBA Ensemble ratings, then prices win totals, seeds, divisions and most-wins futures against the book.

How it works. Each simulated season starts from today's standings and plays every remaining game in date order. A game's margin is the home team's rating minus the away team's, plus home court and the back-to-back term, all fitted by the Ensemble; the home team wins with probability Φ(margin / σ), using the model's fitted game-to-game σ. Before each simulated season every team's rating is nudged by a random amount (the rating uncertainty setting), because ratings this early are uncertain and a team's whole season rises or falls together; without this the simulator would be overconfident on win totals and futures. Current injuries (from ESPN's report, as the model charges them) apply only to each team's next few games, set below, and any team can be adjusted by hand for longer absences, trades or rest plans. Ratings do not update inside a simulated season, and the simulator knows nothing about future injuries, trades or late-season resting, so treat far-off futures with extra caution. Seeds use wins with a random tiebreak (real NBA tiebreakers are not modelled). NBA Cup group games count; the Cup final does not. Futures carry a much bigger built-in house edge than single games and tie money up for months. This is a research tool, not betting advice.
