MLB STAT TOTAL

Today's MLB players, ranked by a simple combined stat total \u2014 grouped by the game they're playing in.

TOTAL = AVG \u2212 OBP \u2212 SLG \u2212 OPS

Season stats from the MLB Stats API. Total is computed exactly as AVG minus OBP minus SLG minus OPS, per current-season numbers. Since OBP, SLG, and OPS are all typically larger numbers than AVG, this total is negative for essentially every player \u2014 it's ranked from highest (least negative) to lowest (most negative) exactly as requested, not adjusted or reinterpreted. Hitters only (non-pitchers with recorded plate appearances this season).
